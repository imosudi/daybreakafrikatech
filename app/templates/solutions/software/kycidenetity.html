Overview

Identity Verification at Scale

Know Your Customer (KYC) compliance is foundational across banking, fintech, healthcare, and telecommunications. Our platform provides unified, developer-friendly infrastructure for multi-point identity verification that meets Nigerian regulatory standards.

  • Virtual NIN (vNIN) for privacy
  • NIN-linked phone verification

Government Documents

  • Driver's licence
  • International passport
  • Voter's card
  • Document OCR + validation
