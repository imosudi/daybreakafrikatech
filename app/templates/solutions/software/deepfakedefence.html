The Challenge

Why Deepfake Defence Matters

Advances in generative AI have made synthetic audio and video indistinguishable from authentic media. For broadcasters, this introduces asymmetric risk: a single manipulated clip can erode decades of trust within minutes.

The Deepfake Defence Hub is an enterprise-grade software framework that embeds automated, real-time detection, verification, and governance directly into newsroom and broadcast workflows.
