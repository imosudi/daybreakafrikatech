Abstract

Migration as Transformation

Cloud adoption is strategic necessity, yet many migrations fail due to rushed execution and inadequate optimisation. This framework treats migration not as an endpoint, but as the foundation for continuous improvement.

Challenges

Key Overlooked Risks

Treating migration as simple relocation rather than business transformation introduces critical risks.

🔗

Undocumented Dependencies

Hidden application links and service interactions leading to unforeseen failures during cutover.

⚡

Workload Complexity

Latency-sensitive or stateful applications requiring specialized handling and architecture redesign.

⚖️

Regulatory Constraints

Compliance, governance, and data sovereignty obligations overlooked in migration planning.

💸

Financial Volatility

Unpredictable and escalating post-migration costs due to poor FinOps practices and resource management.

🚨

Service Disruption

Unexpected downtime during cutover phases impacting critical business operations and user experience.

  • Lift-and-shift for controlled transition
  • Re-platforming for efficiency

⚙️

Post-Migration Optimisation

  • Performance tuning and bottleneck elimination
  • Resource right-sizing and efficiency
  • Availability and resilience improvements
  • Operational automation
