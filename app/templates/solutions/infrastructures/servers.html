High-Availability Architecture

Cluster Design

Robust quorum and predictable failover:

  • Minimum three-node clusters for split-brain prevention
  • Dedicated management and storage networks for bandwidth isolation
  • Predictable failover behaviour ensuring SLO compliance

Failure Domains

Comprehensive fault isolation:

  • Host-level isolation contains hardware/hypervisor failures
  • Storage fault containment decouples compute from storage failures
  • Network path redundancy eliminates single points of failure
